About Aurevia Health Association

Connection that
actually connects.

We started noticing something at conferences. Two kinds of people kept showing up: those already well-connected, performing their networks on LinkedIn and socials, and everyone else, standing at the edges, unsure how to begin. We built Aurevia for the second group. And honestly, for the first group too.

Our Story

We don't do traditional networking.

No crowded rooms. No awkward small talk. No collecting business cards you'll never use. The internet was supposed to bring us closer, and in many ways, it has. But it's also made connection feel more performative, more curated, more exhausting.

"We are here to bring it back to basics. To make it a genuine time where you actually get something out of it."

Whether that's a new professional connection, a job lead, a mentor, a friend, or simply something you didn't know before, that's what Aurevia is for. Too often, people leave conferences, workshops, and networking sessions feeling overwhelmed and disconnected, unsure whether they gained anything of lasting value. We're changing that.

Who We're For

Everyone is already welcome here.

We intentionally design experiences that are welcoming, inclusive, and engaging for people of all backgrounds, personalities, and levels of experience. Whether you're a student just starting out, a young professional finding your footing, a seasoned expert with hard-won wisdom, or a senior looking to stay connected and build digital literacy in healthcare, there is a place at Aurevia for you.

Our events prioritize thoughtful discussion, knowledge sharing, mental health awareness, and real relationship building. We believe the best ideas come from shared experiences, and that not everyone thrives in traditional networking environments. Introverts, newcomers, and first-timers: our programs are designed with you in mind.

What Makes Us Different
